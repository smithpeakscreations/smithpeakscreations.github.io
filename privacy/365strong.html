Privacy Policy

365 Strong Last updated: September 05, 2026

The short version

365 Strong does not collect your data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. Everything you enter — your logged sets, your streak and trophy history, your display name, and any profile photo you choose — is stored only on your device. We have no servers, and we never see any of it.

This Privacy Policy explains how Samuel Smith ("we", "us", or "our"), operating as Smith Peaks Creations, handles information in the 365 Strong mobile application (the "App"). Please read it carefully.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

The App does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide an email address, name, or any other identifying information in order to use it. The App contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking software development kits (SDKs), and no third-party code of any kind. We do not receive usage data, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or location data from the App.

Diagnostics provided by Apple

iOS includes a system-level setting that lets you share diagnostic and usage information with the developers of the apps you use. If you have enabled that setting on your device, Apple may make aggregated crash logs and usage statistics available to us through Apple's developer tools. This information is collected by Apple rather than by the App, is provided to us in aggregate, and is not linked to your identity. You can turn it off at any time in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements. Apple's handling of this information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Information You Store in the App

The App is a tool for tracking push-ups. As you use it, you may enter information such as:

  • The push-up sets you log, including the number of reps and the date and time of each set
  • Your challenge start date, and the streak, milestone, and trophy progress calculated from your logs
  • A display name, if you choose to set one
  • A profile photo, if you choose to add one
  • Your reminder times and notification preferences

All of this information is stored locally on your device, in a private storage area shared only by the App and its own Home Screen widget. It is not transmitted to us, and it is not transmitted to anyone else. We have no ability to access, view, or retrieve it.

Your Name and Profile Photo

The App lets you optionally set a display name and a profile photo so the dashboard feels like yours. Both are entirely optional, and the App works exactly the same without them.

When you choose a profile photo, iOS presents its standard photo picker, and only the specific image you select is shared with the App. The App does not request access to your full photo library, and it cannot browse, read, or upload your photos on its own. The image you pick is saved locally in the App's private storage and never leaves your device. Your display name is stored locally on your device in the same way.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the App schedules local notifications on your device using the iOS notification system — a daily reminder at the time you choose, and optional milestone and streak alerts. These are generated entirely on your device based on data already stored there. No push notification server is involved, and no information about your logs, streaks, or reminders is sent anywhere.

Home Screen and Lock Screen Widget

The App offers a widget that you can add to your Home Screen or Lock Screen. It shows today's total and your current streak, and it can log a set without opening the App.

The widget reads the same on-device data described above and writes back to it when you log from it. Nothing is transmitted anywhere, and no additional information is collected. Because the widget runs as part of the App, iOS gives the two of them a shared private storage area rather than keeping the data inside the App alone; this is a change in where the data sits on your device, not in who can reach it.

One thing worth knowing: if you add the widget to your Lock Screen, your total and streak are visible to anyone who can see your locked screen, without your passcode or Face ID. If you would rather they were not, use the Home Screen widget instead, or do not add a widget at all.

Sharing a Trophy

When you earn a medal, the App offers a share button that opens the standard iOS share sheet with a short line of text naming the trophy you earned. Nothing is shared unless you tap that button and choose a destination yourself. Once you do, the text is handed to whichever app you selected — Messages, Mail, a social app, or anything else — and is then governed by that app's own privacy policy. We are not involved in that step and receive no copy of it.

Rating the App

The Profile screen includes a "Rate the App" option that asks iOS to present Apple's standard review prompt. The prompt, and any rating or review you write, is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see which users were shown the prompt or how any individual responded. Apple's handling of that information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Internet Access

The App does not send your information over the internet. It makes no network requests to our servers, because we do not operate any servers for the App. The only outbound actions are ones you start yourself: opening this policy in your browser, sharing a trophy, or contacting support.

Contacting Support

The App includes an option to email us for support, which opens your device's mail composer with our support address and a version tag already filled in. Nothing is sent until you send it. If you do choose to write to us, we will see your email address and whatever you include in your message, and we use that only to respond to you. We do not add you to any mailing list.

iCloud

The current version of the App does not sync your data to iCloud; your information stays on the single device where you entered it. If a future version of the App offers iCloud sync, your data would be stored in your own private iCloud database under your Apple Account, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Data in a private iCloud database is accessible only to you — we would not be able to see or access it. Any such feature would be optional, and this policy would be updated before it ships.

Health Data

The App does not connect to Apple Health. It does not read from or write to HealthKit, and it has no access to your health or fitness records outside of the push-up sets you log directly in the App. The App is an activity tracker, not a medical or treatment-focused tool, and nothing in it is intended as medical advice.

Purchases

The current version of the App does not offer in-app purchases or subscriptions. If a future version does, purchases would be processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We would never receive or have access to your payment card details, billing address, or Apple Account credentials. Apple's handling of that information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

How Your Information Is Shared

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose your information, because we never receive it in the first place. There are no service providers, advertising partners, data brokers, or affiliates with access to your data.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because your data lives only on your device, you are in full control of it. You can edit or delete individual log entries from within the App at any time, and every total, streak, and trophy recalculates from what remains. Deleting the App from your device permanently removes all of the data it stored, including your name and profile photo. We hold no copy of your data and therefore have nothing to delete on our end.

If your device is backed up through iCloud Backup or an encrypted computer backup, the App's data may be included in that backup. Those backups are managed by Apple and by you, not by us.

Security

Your information is protected by the security of your device. Data stored by the App resides in a sandboxed storage area shared by the App and its own widget, which iOS isolates from apps by other developers, and is protected by your device passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID along with the device's built-in encryption. We recommend keeping a passcode enabled and keeping iOS up to date.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the App collects no information at all, no personal information from a child can be transmitted to us.

Your Rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, delete, and port your personal data, and to know whether it is sold or shared. Because we do not collect or process any of your personal data, we hold nothing to disclose, correct, delete, or transfer. You can exercise complete control over your information directly within the App and on your device. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA, and we have never done so.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom and believe your privacy rights have been infringed, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the App gains new features. When we do, we will post the revised policy on this page and update the "Last updated" date above. Material changes to how information is handled will be reflected here before the corresponding feature is released. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Who We Are

365 Strong is published by Samuel Smith, an independent developer based in Utah, United States, operating as Smith Peaks Creations. For the purposes of the GDPR and similar privacy laws, Samuel Smith is the data controller responsible for the App. In practice, the App is designed so that there is no personal data for us to control: we operate no servers and receive no information from it.
