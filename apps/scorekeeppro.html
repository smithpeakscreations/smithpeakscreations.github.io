ScoreKeep Pro

Keep score for any game — cards, board games, trivia night.

Free · Requires iOS 17.6 or later · Version 1.2.0

What it does

Set up a game in seconds

Up to 10 players and up to 10 rounds, with custom round names so the scoresheet matches the game you’re actually playing.

A leaderboard that keeps up

Standings update in real time as scores go in, so nobody has to add up a column mid-game.

Save your favourite setups

Keep the games you play often as templates and start the next round without rebuilding the whole thing.

Nothing gets lost

Progress saves automatically, so you can put the phone down mid-game and pick it back up later.

Works anywhere

No connection needed. ScoreKeep Pro works offline, at the kitchen table or the back of a cabin.

Questions

What does ScoreKeep Pro cost?

It’s free on the App Store.

How many players and rounds does it support?

Up to 10 players and up to 10 rounds per game, with custom round names.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. ScoreKeep Pro works fully offline.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. ScoreKeep Pro runs on iPhone and iPad, and requires iOS 17.6 or later.

What happens if I close the app mid-game?

Game progress saves automatically and will be waiting when you reopen it.
